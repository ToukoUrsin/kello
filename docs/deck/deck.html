Why you can trust it

Every number says how it was made, and opens the page it came from.

Measured, estimated or assumed

Each figure carries one of three labels, its period, the page and the sentence it was read from.

1,628 values read from registry reports

Finnish county cancer data has no API. Gemini read the PDFs and charts; 1,607 values were found again in the source, 21 are shown as unverified.

Checked against the web, live

For each action: is it already running in this county? Searches and page reads go through the Oxylabs Web API; a quote that is not word for word in the page is dropped. The answer changes the plan.

What it cannot show

No trial has measured these effects, so every range starts at zero. Cancer stage is not published by county. Our first model, a clock from symptom to treatment, did not match outcomes; the app says so.

The ask

A six-month pilot with Lapland and the Oulu university hospital.

  1. Week 1: ask the university hospital which of the six actions already run. The web check says four partly do.
  2. Weeks 1–8: audit 50 charts for €8,000. Settles stage at diagnosis, missing records and drug treatment.
  3. Months 3–6: start the two actions the audit says are new: a chest surgeon reviews every decision not to operate; every patient who cannot be operated is referred for precise radiotherapy.
  4. Counted in six months: 50 charts audited, about 71 patients seen, about 22 decisions not to operate reviewed (cancer not spread), decisions changed, referrals made. Model estimate for 2028: 18.5% to 20.5% treated to cure.
