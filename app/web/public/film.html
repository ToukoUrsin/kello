Film mode

  1. Click Show my lines. A small window opens that stays on top: it shows what to say. Drag it where you like.
  2. In the recorder choose Window or Current tab and pick this Kello window. Not the whole screen: then the lines would be in the film.
  3. Press F for full screen, start recording, press S. After 3, 2, 1 read the underlined word. The demo plays by itself.
